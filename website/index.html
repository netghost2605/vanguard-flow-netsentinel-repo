Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Packet capture needs Wireshark installed (free, from wireshark.org) — NetSentinel uses it rather than bundling it. Speed testing needs a speed-test command-line tool on the machine.

Your data

Everything is measured and stored on your own machine. No account, no telemetry, nothing uploaded. The reports are files you own.

Honest limits

This measures the connection as your machine sees it. That is a strong record of what you receive, but it is not a regulator-certified test, and it cannot diagnose faults inside your provider's network.
